# BusinessNotes — Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Continuity and foundation
- Establish a shared system context.
- Establish product vision and terminology.
- Establish current-state and handoff records.
- Establish decision and audit records.
- Keep GitHub as the shared source of truth.
- Never merge unreviewed work directly into main.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Authentication and profiles
- Companies and memberships
- Core feed
- Search
- Basic notifications
- Core data model
- Security and authorization baseline

## Phase 2 — Business network
- Opportunities
- Projects
- Products/services
- Events
- Business matching
- Messaging and professional connections
- Company business pages

## Phase 3 — Media and trust
- News/articles/media
- Sources and evidence
- Editorial workflow
- Corrections/takedowns/appeals
- Verification
- Moderation and governance
- Public SEO

## Phase 4 — AI
- Source-grounded AI
- Content assistant
- Search and discovery
- Recommendations
- Matching
- Content transformation/distribution

## Phase 5 — Intelligence
- Business intelligence
- Market/industry analysis
- Opportunity intelligence
- Risk analysis
- Analytics
- Smart alerts

## Phase 6 — Ecosystem
- Enterprise capabilities
- APIs/integrations
- Monetization
- Advanced verification/reputation
- Expanded business and institutional workflows

## Execution rule
Roadmap phases describe direction, not permission to skip engineering fundamentals. Before advancing, validate the existing phase and dependencies.

Every roadmap change must be documented in a decision record.
