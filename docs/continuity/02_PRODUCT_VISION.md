# BusinessNotes — Product Vision

## Position
BusinessNotes is designed as a professional business information network where information can move toward insight, connection, opportunity, and business activity.

## Core experience
Users should be able to:
- discover reliable business information;
- understand context through source-grounded content and AI assistance;
- connect with relevant people, companies, projects, and opportunities;
- publish and distribute business content;
- discover and evaluate opportunities;
- communicate and collaborate;
- build professional identity and reputation;
- use verification and evidence to increase trust;
- access analytics and business intelligence.

## Opportunity model
Supported opportunity categories include:
Investment, Partnership, JV, Supplier, Buyer, Distributor, Project, Acquisition, Franchise, Technology, Licensing, Funding, Procurement, Tender.

## Trust model
Trust is a system layer, not a decorative badge. Verification, source records, evidence, editorial history, corrections, takedowns, appeals, moderation, privacy, audit logs, and reputation should work together.

## AI role
AI should assist users with:
- drafting and transforming content;
- summarization;
- source-grounded answers;
- market/industry/competitor/opportunity/risk analysis;
- search;
- recommendations;
- matching;
- multilingual content;
- distribution formats.

AI output must distinguish, where relevant:
**Fact / Source / Analysis / Opinion**

AI must not invent evidence, verification, business opportunities, or credentials.

## Content engine
One source content item may be transformed into multiple distribution formats such as article, social post, graphic/infographic concept, video script/caption, WhatsApp message, LinkedIn post, X post, and newsletter, while preserving provenance and meaning.

## User experience principle
Complex intelligence belongs inside the system; the public interface should remain understandable.

**POWERFUL INSIDE — SIMPLE OUTSIDE**

## Long-term ecosystem
The platform can evolve through:
Foundation → Business → Media → AI → Intelligence → Ecosystem

Each phase must build on the prior phase rather than create an isolated product.
